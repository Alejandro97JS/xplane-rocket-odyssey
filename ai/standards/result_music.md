# Music of the result

The project owner asked for this on 2026-11-14. Follow it. Changing it needs the owner's approval.

The launch already has sound, from the countdown until the rocket reaches space. The music below is added to it.

## Rules

1. When a mission succeeds, music plays that recalls the music of the film *2001: A Space Odyssey*.
2. When a mission fails, music plays that recalls the music video games typically play when the player loses.
3. The music must be free of copyright.
4. The success music is short: only the opening of the fanfare, about 6 seconds of music. The owner found the first version, about 9 seconds, too long and asked to keep only its first half.

## Not approved yet

The tunes, when they play and how they are muted were decided while building them. The owner has not approved them yet. They are described, with the reasons, in `ai/self_documentation/result_music.md`. In short:

- Both tunes are written for this game and synthesised in the browser, so there are no audio files and no recordings. The success tune recalls the film without quoting its music, which is still under copyright in Spain.
- The music plays when the animation of the launch reaches its end, and goes on into the debrief. A player who skipped the animation, or saw the flight as a list, hears it when the debrief opens. It plays once per launch, unless the animation is watched to the end again.
- One sound setting mutes the launch and the music, and it is remembered. The debrief has its own sound button, at the top of the page.
