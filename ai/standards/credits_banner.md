# Credits banner

The project owner asked for this on 2026-11-15. Follow it. Changing it needs the owner's approval.

## Rules

1. Every screen of the game has a thin banner at the bottom.
2. The banner says that the game is made by the XPlane team.
3. The banner says that the game takes part in the NASA Space Apps Challenge, at the Salamanca (Spain) local event.
